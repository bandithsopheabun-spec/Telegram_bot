Note: 
 Identification Documents include Cambodian ID Card, Passport, Cambodian Driver License, Government ID Card and 
Civil Servant Card. 
 For listed company, it is not required to obtain identification document of shareholders and Board of Director. 
 Document Certification: 
 The legal documents including company and individual identity documents must be presented to the Bank 
in original otherwise they must be certified true copied by: 
o Public notary: if the public notary is from foreign country, they must then be certified by their relevant 
Embassy in Cambodia. This certification shall be the obligation of the customer. 
o Cambodian Embassy in the relevant country. 
o Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the relevant country and then certified by the Cambodian Ministry 
of Foreign Affairs. 
 The foreign company legal documents must be always certified as mentioned above. 
 The partner must present and sign/thump print Authorization Letter/ Resolution and other signatories 
documents in front of the Bank’s staff otherwise those documents must be certified as mentioned above.

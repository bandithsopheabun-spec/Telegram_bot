About Us

Blessing.Kh is a social media marketing (SMM) reselling service based in Cambodia, operating through a Telegram bot and companion web ordering portal. We provide engagement services (likes, views, followers) for TikTok, Telegram, and Facebook to individual and business customers who want to grow their social media presence.

Customers top up an in-app wallet using Bakong KHQR (and other supported local payment methods), then place orders for the service package of their choice directly through the bot or website. Orders are processed and fulfilled by our support team, with order status and history available to customers at all times.

Privacy Policy

We collect only the information necessary to operate the service:

  • Telegram account information (numeric user ID, username, first name) — used to identify your account, wallet balance, and order history.
  • Order details (target link, package, price) — used solely to fulfil your order.
  • Payment/deposit records (amount, timestamp, status) — used to credit your wallet and for our own accounting.

We do not sell or share customer information with third parties, except where required to process a payment (e.g. with Bakong/NBC's payment verification systems) or where required by law. Data is stored in a secured database and is not publicly accessible.

Refund Policy

  • Deposits credited to your wallet in error, or duplicate deposits, will be corrected/refunded to your wallet upon verification by our support team.
  • Orders that have already started processing are not eligible for a refund, except where our support team cancels the order (e.g. due to an invalid/incorrect link), in which case the order amount is returned to your wallet balance.
  • Wallet balance is not withdrawable to a bank account or cash — it may only be used to place orders on Blessing.Kh.
  • To request a refund review, contact @Blessing_Kh_Supports with your Order ID or Deposit ID.
